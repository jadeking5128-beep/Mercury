# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Mercury contributors and community members are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions on their merits.
- Be considerate of differing experience levels and perspectives.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections professionally.
- Respect privacy; do not publish secrets, personal data, or target information.
- Keep security discussions responsible and within authorized testing boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, hate speech, or sexualized abuse.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Threats, stalking, or targeted disruption.
- Encouraging unauthorized access or harmful use of project tooling.

## Enforcement

Report violations through the repository's private reporting options or contact a maintainer privately. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary and should handle reports confidentially and fairly.
